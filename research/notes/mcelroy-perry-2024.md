# McElroy & Perry (2024)

**Citation and access.** Elizabeth E. McElroy and Samuel L. Perry, “The Gender Gap in Partnered Orgasm: A Scoping Review of Evidence with Graphical Comparisons,” *The Journal of Sex Research* 61(9), 1298–1315 (2024), [DOI 10.1080/00224499.2024.2390672](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224499.2024.2390672). **Question and design.** Scoping review of roughly 30 years of research on orgasm disparities between men and women in heterosexual encounters. The public PDF reports 49 included studies, with 41 shown in one or more figures. It compares measurement techniques, behaviors, and social contexts. Its Table 1 inventories studies and sample characteristics; Figure 1 records study selection. This is a synthesis, not a new participant dataset; no single population or denominator applies to every figure.

**Outcomes and findings.** The abstract distinguishes occurrence and frequency. It reports that women’s orgasm rates are higher when behaviors involving specific clitoral stimulation are included and that gaps are especially pronounced with less partner familiarity, experience, or commitment. It also notes differences across masturbation and partner genders. These are review-level interpretations, not directly extracted numeric observations.

**Limits.** The graphical comparisons draw from studies with different questions, populations, dates, and definitions. The review notes that some source papers assumed heterosexuality rather than measuring it. Before reusing a chart value, locate its underlying primary study and exact figure/table. No common confidence interval or participant denominator exists for the review, and no CSV was created from its graphics.
