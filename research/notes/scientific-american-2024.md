# Scientific American editorial reference

**Citation and access.** Meghan McDonough, “How to Close the ‘Orgasm Gap’ for Heterosexual Couples,” edited by Tulika Bose, *Scientific American*, February 21, 2024. [Public podcast page and transcript](https://www.scientificamerican.com/podcast/episode/how-to-close-the-orgasm-gap-for-heterosexual-couples/), accessed 2026-11-02. This is journalism, not an original data source.

**Argument and structure, in original summary.** The episode introduces the heterosexual orgasm gap, then follows a historical sequence: early physiological research, Shere Hite’s challenge to intercourse-centered definitions of sex, a later survey finding by sexual orientation, and contemporary research and counseling around stimulation, communication, and social norms. It ends with discussion of agency and context beyond technique. The speakers include a journalist, researchers or historical recordings, a product cofounder, and a sex therapist. This structure is an editorial reference only; it is not the student’s article outline.

**Research use.** The episode points to Frederick et al. for orientation figures and other studies for techniques and communication. Any statistic or causal implication should be checked at its primary source before appearing in the student’s article. The transcript itself is not stored here, and no audio or images were downloaded.

**Limit.** As a narrated selection of research and interviews, it does not supply a single common sample or data table. Its claims should not be used as numeric observations in `data/raw/`.
