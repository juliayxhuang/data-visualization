## Interpretation

- Student draft, “Relationship context”: Armstrong et al. report whether orgasm occurred in a particular reported event. Consider changing “reported orgasm frequency” to “reported orgasm occurrence” before treating the paragraph as final. The first-hookup and relationship-event percentages come from different contexts and should not imply a causal effect of relationship status.
- Which outcome is central: orgasm frequency, occurrence in an event, enjoyment, sexual satisfaction, or relationship satisfaction?
- Can the article discuss partner familiarity without implying that relationship status itself caused an outcome?
- Which study categories need an explicit note about exclusion of trans, nonbinary, or other identities, or limited orientation categories?
- Would placing age and relationship-structure studies in one narrative imply false comparability?
- Which claims in the Scientific American editorial reference should be verified against their cited primary research before reuse?
