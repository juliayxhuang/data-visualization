# Possible article outline — draft, not a fixed thesis

1. **Opening question.** What aspect of the orgasm gap should this article investigate? Leave the claim and title open.
2. **Measurement.** Define the exact outcome before showing a number: orgasm in the last event, usual frequency, estimated percentage of encounters, enjoyment, sexual satisfaction, or relationship satisfaction.
3. **Possible evidence paths.** Sexual orientation (Frederick); age (Gesselman); hookup and relationship context (Armstrong); relationship structure and satisfaction (Anderson, Morar-Bolba, Mitchell). These are separate studies and possible sections, not one dataset or required sequence.
4. **Interpretation and limits.** Explain sampling, subgroup definitions, uncertainty, and why observed associations do not establish causes.
5. **Open ending.** Return to the student’s chosen question without a predetermined conclusion.
