# Open connections and checks

This page is a queue of research and documentation questions, not an article outline. The fuller [research question list](../research/open-questions.md) remains in place.

## Research checks

- Which values in a future visual have been verified from a primary paper, and which are from a secondary account? Check the [source map](sources.md) and [bibliography](../research/sources.md).
- Are a proposed pair of images encoding the same outcome, time frame, population, and unit? Check [measures](measures.md) before suggesting a numerical comparison.
- For any new age chart, extract directly reported age-group values and uncertainty rather than assigning the overall range to groups.
- If relationship-structure studies enter the project, keep sexual satisfaction, relationship satisfaction, and orgasm outcomes distinct; check their recruitment methods and definitions.
- If the [Hensel experiments](experiments.md#pairing-illustration-series) are described publicly, do the captions make clear that these are overlapping, self-reported technique-use measures during penetration?

## Process documentation

- Which generated outputs should be kept as experiments, and which, if any, should become part of the scrolling experience? This is the student's creative decision.
- When sound and motion experiments are ready, what data do they use, what coding rule changes the output, and what is a reader meant to learn or notice? Record the student's answer before adding a synthesis here.
- Is a generated image intended to communicate a study result, reveal a coding choice, or both? Clarify per artifact rather than assuming one role for the whole series.
