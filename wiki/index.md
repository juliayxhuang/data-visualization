# Project research wiki

Agent-maintained working map, started 2026-11-08. This is a research aid, not a draft of the scrolling story or a selected visual direction. The [wiki workflow](AGENTS.md) explains how to keep it current.

## Start here

- [Source map](sources.md) — each publication's local note, extracted data, and access status.
- [What the measures mean](measures.md) — distinctions that matter when connecting studies or making images.
- [Experiment map](experiments.md) — the data and decisions behind the existing code-generated outputs.
- [Age image concepts](../experiments/processing-age/CONCEPTS.md) — ungenerated options for the Gesselman model results and editable styling controls.
- [Open connections and checks](questions.md) — unresolved research and interpretation questions, without a proposed thesis.
- [Change log](log.md) — chronological wiki maintenance record.

## Source of truth outside the wiki

- [Annotated bibliography](../research/sources.md) and [source-specific notes](../research/notes/) hold publication details, wording, limits, and retrieval status.
- [Data dictionary](../data/README.md) explains the separate [study CSV files](../data/raw/).
- [Decision record](../docs/decisions.md) records the student's choices and agent contributions.
- [Experiment folder](../experiments/) holds sketches, inputs, outputs, and their READMEs.

The CSV files are manual transcriptions of published aggregate values. Original papers are generally linked rather than copied into the repository. Follow the cited source before making a new factual claim.
